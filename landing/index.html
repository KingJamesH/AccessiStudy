Everything you need to read comfortably

Most accessibility tools fix one thing. WebAble brings the essentials together in one simple popup.

Adjustable text size

Scale text up from 80% to 200% so you never have to squint at a tiny font again.

Dyslexia-friendly font

Switch any page to OpenDyslexic, a typeface designed to make letters easier to tell apart.

Letter & line spacing

Open up the space between letters and lines to reduce crowding and keep your place.

High contrast mode

Force crisp light-on-dark text to cut glare and sharpen low-contrast pages.

Applies instantly

Every change shows up on the page as you make it — drag a slider and watch it update live.

Private by design

Your preferences are saved locally with Chrome storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
